Dear Editors,

We submit our manuscript, "GShare: Lossless Preemptible Sharing of Exclusive GPUs via an
Occupancy Memory Hierarchy," for consideration as a regular research article in Future
Generation Computer Systems.

Multi-tenant GPU clusters bill tenants for occupying a card rather than for using it, and on
Kubernetes a card's occupancy is bound to the lifetime of the pod that holds it. This coupling
forces a lossy choice: keep paying for an idle exclusive card, or stop the pod and lose the
workload's in-GPU state. Existing approaches do not close this gap -- space-sharing cannot give
any job a full card under SM saturation, and the Kubernetes-native checkpoint/restore path cannot
resume a GPU losslessly because the restored checkpoint must re-enter a kubelet-owned sandbox
network namespace that is destroyed with the pod.

Our central idea is to model GPU occupancy as a position in a memory hierarchy -- VRAM, host RAM,
disk/NVMe, and a durable cold tier -- through which a session's working set moves losslessly, with
demotion/promotion as the inter-tier paging policy. By keeping the pod alive, GShare sidesteps the
Kubernetes restore wall and delivers a capability space-sharing structurally cannot: lossless,
application-transparent, preemptible sharing of exclusive GPU cards on the native stack. We build
GShare on a production GPU Kubernetes stack and validate every tier and transition on RTX-4090
hardware, including a measured lossless disk-tier round-trip, real two-node multi-tenant
yield/borrow/reclaim, a cross-validated cluster simulator, and a real-trace-grounded analysis that
we deliberately report as an honest bound rather than a single inflated figure.

We believe this work fits FGCS's scope in cloud and distributed resource management, virtualization,
and multi-tenant infrastructure. Its contribution is a systems one -- an abstraction (occupancy as a
memory hierarchy), the architecture and economic/paging policy built on it, and a real, thoroughly
measured implementation -- and we have taken care throughout to bound claims to what the
measurements support.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under
consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submission and have
no conflicts of interest to declare. A reproducibility artifact (scripts, raw data, and figure
sources) accompanies the paper.
